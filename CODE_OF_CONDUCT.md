# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We want this to be a place where anyone can contribute a bug report, a documentation fix or a new
converter without being made to feel unwelcome. We pledge to make participation harassment-free for
everyone, regardless of age, body size, visible or invisible disability, ethnicity, sex
characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic
status, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Our standards

Examples of behaviour that builds this project up:

- Being respectful of differing opinions, viewpoints and experiences
- Giving and gracefully accepting constructive feedback
- Focusing on what is best for the project and its users
- Crediting other people's work — this project exists because of upstream ConvertX and 20+ tools

Examples of unacceptable behaviour:

- Sexualised language or imagery, and sexual attention of any kind
- Trolling, insulting or derogatory comments, and personal or political attacks
- Public or private harassment
- Publishing someone's private information without their explicit permission
- Other conduct which could reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional setting

## Scope

This applies in all project spaces (issues, pull requests, discussions, reviews) and when an
individual is representing the project in public.

## Enforcement

Maintainers are responsible for clarifying and enforcing these standards. They may remove, edit or
reject comments, commits, code and issues that do not align with this Code of Conduct, and will
explain their decision when appropriate.

If you experience or witness unacceptable behaviour, report it by opening a confidential report
through the repository's **Security → Advisories** page, or by contacting a maintainer directly.
Reports are reviewed promptly and handled discreetly. Maintainers who do not follow or enforce this
Code of Conduct in good faith may face temporary or permanent consequences.
